About Us

Third-generation Ottawa renovation contractor, based in Orleans.

Unleashing the Power of Athletic Precision in Home Renovations – At Home Upgraders Ottawa

At Home Upgraders Ottawa Contractors, we bring an athlete's ethos to the home renovation world. Founded by a third-generation Ottawa Contractor with a background as a competitive baseball player — and now my son, the fourth generation, has worked alongside me this year — we've learned that the discipline of sports translates perfectly into our home renovation projects. Just as in baseball, where training, attention to detail, and a relentless drive to excel set us apart, we apply the same principles to upgrading your home. Our philosophy, inherited from our grandfather, is simple yet profound: “If you don't take the time to do it right, you'll never do it at all.” We believe in doing it right the first time, sparing no detail, even if it takes longer.
