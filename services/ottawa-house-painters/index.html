Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your interior painting services stand out from the rest?

We pay close attention to prep and finish so the painted surface holds up over time. We arrive with color samples and a color wheel to help with paint selection, and we exclusively use high-quality paints like Dulux, though we're flexible and can work with your preferred brand, including Home Depot and Benjamin Moore options.

How does the complimentary colour consultation work?

Our complimentary colour consultation is a personalized service to help you choose the right colour. We guide you through palettes, comparisons, and recommendations so you feel confident in your choice before we start.

Do you offer specialized services for different areas of the home, such as kitchens and condominiums?

Yes, we offer a range of specialized interior painting services tailored to the unique qualities of different areas in your home. Whether it's a condo with a unique layout requiring a careful touch or a kitchen that needs durable, cleanable paint on cabinets and walls, our experienced team applies their craft with precision. And if after completing one room you decide to continue the transformation, we're more than happy to plan the next stages of your home's visual upgrade, always aligning with your vision and schedule.
