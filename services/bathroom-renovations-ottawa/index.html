Our Comprehensive Ottawa Bathroom Remodel Services

  • Subfloor Replacement and Leveling: To ensure longevity and safety, we start by carefully removing the subfloor for plumbing access, followed by professional reinstalling and leveling.
  • Waterproofing Measures: Bathrooms demand resilience. Before tile or vinyl flooring goes down, we install a waterproofing membrane to protect the subfloor against moisture.
  • Tile Layout: For tiled floors and showers, you can preview your layout and vein-matching before we start with TileMatchMaker, our free tile-planning tool.
  • Radiant Heated Floors: Step into comfort with our Ditra-heat mat installation, preparing your floors for a sophisticated electrical wire heating system that offers warmth and luxury.
